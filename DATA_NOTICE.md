# Data and source material

This release provides the study's adapted questions, reference answers, generated model responses and derived measurements for inspection and reproducibility. References to source manuals and casebooks identify the material used in the thesis. They do not transfer rights in those publications.

Full books, scanned PDFs, verbatim evidence collections, retrieved source contexts and raw provider responses are not distributed. Readers need their own lawful access to the cited sources to repeat source-dependent experiments. The public projection preserves question and answer text while removing internal authoring metadata and operational paths.

Clinical vignettes are adapted teaching cases from published casebooks, not exported clinical records from a healthcare institution. Their inclusion does not make this repository a clinical decision-support system. Scores describe agreement with the study references under the recorded assessment protocol.

Please cite the accompanying master's thesis and this repository when discussing its results. No blanket licence to the underlying third-party manuals, model weights or casebooks is granted here.
